## Process choice

### How stable and binding are the requirements?
The requirements are not fully stable because the client only gave a short initial request and important details are still unclear. The requirements may change once lab staff start giving feedback.

### How quickly can real feedback arrive?
Feedback can arrive quickly because lab staff and students who borrow equipment are easy to reach and can test early versions of the system.

### What does failure cost?
Failure would mainly cause operational problems, such as equipment remaining untracked or overdue. The cost is important, but it is not safety-critical, so improvements can be made gradually.
### How many pieces must move together?
The system has several connected parts, such as recording loans, tracking due dates, recording returns, and identifying overdue equipment. However, these features can be developed and tested in small increments.

Verdict:
Use a short-increment, iterative process because the requirements may change and frequent feedback from lab staff can help improve the product.
